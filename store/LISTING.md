# Bark Break — Chrome Web Store listing

## Name
Bark Break — Browser Dog Pet

## Short description
A real dog that lives on your websites — finds treasures, remembers how you play, and occasionally causes harmless chaos.

## Detailed description

Meet Biscuit. After you approve a website, Biscuit walks in along the bottom of the page — sits, sleeps, follows the pointer, and sometimes asks for a snack, a toy, or a two-minute walk.

**Lives on the page**
- Walking, resting, sleeping, and looking around
- Pet, nose boop, belly rub, feed, water, and play
- Ball fetch, squeaky toy, and tug rope
- Soft walkies reminder after you’ve been active a while
- Move, hide, or pause when Biscuit is in the way

**Surprises without guilt**
- Combination reactions (order matters)
- Rare short events and ultra-rare moments
- Found-object collection in the toolbar popup
- Four moods: Relaxed, Playful, Curious, Snack-minded
- Light local memory (favourite food, toy, corner)

No account, shop, coins, or leaderboard. Preferences and finds stay on your device. Bark Break does not read page content. Sound is off until you enable it.

## Single purpose
Bark Break shows a friendly virtual dog companion on websites you approve, with simple care interactions, optional short break reminders, and light local progression.

## Permission justifications

| Permission | Justification |
| --- | --- |
| `storage` | Saves local preferences, mood, finds, and light daily counters |
| `alarms` | Ends the optional 2-minute walk timer reliably |
| `scripting` | Draws the dog overlay on approved pages |
| `activeTab` | Helps when choosing the current site |
| Optional host access | Requested for the sites where Biscuit may appear |
