# Privacy Policy — Bark Break

**Last updated:** 11 August 2026

Bark Break places a local dog companion overlay on websites you approve.

## Data stored locally
- Preferences (sound, size, attention frequency, visibility, pause)
- Approved site origins (if you choose specific sites)
- Light daily counters (attention requests, engaged browsing minutes for the optional break reminder)

## Not collected
- Page text, forms, passwords, searches, messages, or video titles
- Full browsing history
- Accounts or identity
- Remote analytics

## Network
No browsing or pet data is sent to a remote server.
