# Privacy Policy — Paw Pause

**Last updated:** 12 August 2026

Paw Pause places a local companion overlay on websites you approve.

## Data stored locally
- Preferences (sound, size, attention frequency, visibility, pause)
- Companion name, look, personality, mood, and found-object collection
- Approved site origins (if you choose specific sites)
- Light daily counters (attention requests, engaged browsing minutes for the optional break reminder)

## Not collected
- Page text, forms, passwords, searches, messages, or video titles
- Full browsing history
- Accounts or identity
- Remote analytics

## Network
No browsing or companion data is sent to a remote server. Optional sound samples are packaged with the extension and loaded locally only.
